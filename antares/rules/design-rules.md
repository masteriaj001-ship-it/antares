# Design Rules

## Visual Identity
- Editorial minimalism
- Dark cinematic aesthetic
- Strong typography
- Clean spacing hierarchy
- Avoid generic AI startup visuals

## Avoid
- Overused gradients
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Floating dashboard clichés
- Generic SaaS hero sections

## Prioritize
- Composition
- Rhythm
- Atmosphere
- Negative space
- Subtle interactions
